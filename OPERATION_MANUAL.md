# MinutesFlow — Manual of Operation

**Product:** MinutesFlow AI  
**Website:** https://minutesflow.com  
**Also available at:**  
- https://minutesflow.web.app  
- https://meeting-minutes--gen-lang-client-0135145658.asia-southeast1.hosted.app  

**Support:** support@minutesflow.com  

This manual explains how to use MinutesFlow day to day: sign in, buy credits, record meetings, generate minutes, and download results.

**In the app:** open **Operation Manual** from the Home dashboard, the sidebar, Settings → Help, or the login page footer.

---

## 1. What MinutesFlow does

MinutesFlow turns a spoken meeting into:

1. **Structured Minutes** — summary, discussion points, decisions, and action items  
2. **Verbatim English Transcript** — full word-for-word English text  

You can **copy**, **download as `.txt`**, or **read aloud** the results.

Works on **phone and computer** in a web browser. No app install required.

---

## 2. Before you start

### Requirements

- A modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox recommended)
- A **Google account** (for sign-in)
- Microphone permission (for live recording)
- Stable internet connection
- At least **1 meeting credit** to generate minutes (unless your account is unlimited)

### Recommended device setup

| Device | Tip |
|--------|-----|
| Phone | Keep the browser tab open while recording; avoid locking the screen for very long meetings if possible |
| Computer | Use Chrome/Edge; allow microphone when prompted |
| Meeting room | Place the phone/laptop near the speakers |

---

## 3. Sign in

1. Open https://minutesflow.com  
2. Tap **Sign in with Google**  
3. Choose your Google account and allow access  

**Notes**

- Guest / sandbox login is **not** available  
- One Google email = one MinutesFlow account  
- Privacy Policy and Terms are linked on the login screen and in Settings  

If you see `auth/unauthorized-domain`, make sure you are using an official URL above (not a random preview link).

---

## 4. Main screens (navigation)

After sign-in you will see tabs:

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **Home / Dashboard** | Overview and quick status |
| **Record** | Record live audio or upload a file, then generate minutes |
| **History** | Past meetings, redo, download recording |

On mobile these appear as icons at the bottom.

## 5. Credits and pricing

### How credits work

- **1 credit = 1 paid Generate** for a meeting  
- Credits are **pay-as-you-go** (no subscription)  
- Credits **do not expire**  
- After the first paid Generate, **Redo is free for 24 hours** for that meeting  

### Current prices (MYR)

| Package | Price |
|---------|-------|
| 1 credit | **RM29** |
| 5 credits | **RM145** |
| 10 credits | **RM290** |

### How to buy credits

1. Open the **Credits** tab  
2. Choose **1**, **5**, or **10** credits  
3. Tap **Buy**  
4. Complete Stripe checkout  
5. Return to the app — credits appear on your account (may take a few seconds)

If checkout was cancelled, no charge is made and no credits are added.

## 6. Record a meeting (live)

1. Go to **Record**  
2. (Optional) Enter a **meeting title**  
3. Tap **Start Recording** and allow the microphone  
4. Speak / run the meeting as usual  
5. Watch the timer and **Cloud Buffering** status (up to about **5 hours**)  
6. Tap **Stop** when finished  

### After stop

The app saves the recording to your history.  
**Important:** Saving the recording alone does **not** use a credit.  
A credit is used when you tap **Generate Minutes**.

### Recording tips

- Keep the mic close to people talking  
- Avoid heavy background noise  
- Do not close the browser tab during recording  
- Very short / silent captures may be rejected  

---

## 7. Upload an audio file

1. Go to **Record**  
2. Choose **Upload** / select an audio file from your device  
3. Wait for upload to finish  
4. Tap **Generate Minutes** when ready  

### Upload limits

- Maximum file size: about **500 MB**  
- Supported common browser/audio formats (for example WebM, MP3, WAV, M4A — depending on device)

---

## 8. Generate minutes

1. Open the saved recording on **Record** or **History**  
2. Confirm you have at least **1 credit**  
3. Tap **Generate Minutes**  
4. Wait while AI processes the audio  

Processing can take several minutes for long meetings. Keep the tab open.

### What you get

- **Structured Minutes** tab  
- **Verbatim English Transcript** tab  

Non-English speech is translated into English where possible.

### If no speech is detected

You may see a “no speech” style message. Check the mic/file and try again. In some cases a credit may not be charged when no usable speech is found — follow the on-screen message.

---

## 9. Copy, download, and read aloud

With minutes or transcript open:

| Action | What it does |
|--------|----------------|
| **Copy Raw** | Copies the text to clipboard |
| **Download** | Saves a plain **`.txt`** file (opens on phone and PC) |
| **Read aloud** | Speaks the text (browser speech) |

Download filenames look like:

- `meeting-title-minutes.txt`  
- `meeting-title-transcript.txt`  

---

## 10. History

Open **History** to:

- View past meetings  
- Open minutes / transcript again  
- **Redo** generation (free within **24 hours** after first paid Generate; after that, redo needs another credit)  
- Download the original recording (while it is still stored)  
- Delete meetings you no longer need  

### Storage note

Recordings are kept for up to about **90 days** (then may be removed). Minutes text in your history may still remain depending on account data — download important results early if you need a permanent local copy.

## 11. Settings and account

In **Settings** you can typically:

- See account / credit status  
- Open **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service**  
- **Delete account** (permanent — use carefully)

Sign out from the account menu when finished on a shared computer.

## 12. Typical workflow (checklist)

1. Sign in with Google  
2. Buy credits if balance is 0  
3. Open **Record** → Start Recording (or Upload)  
4. Stop when the meeting ends  
5. Tap **Generate Minutes** (uses 1 credit)  
6. Review Structured Minutes + Transcript  
7. Copy / Download `.txt`  
8. Optional: Redo within 24 hours if needed (free)

## 13. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| Cannot sign in | Use Chrome; allow pop-ups; open an official URL listed above |
| Microphone blocked | Browser site settings → allow Microphone for minutesflow.com |
| Recording stops early | Keep tab active; check battery / screen lock settings |
| Generate fails / no credits | Buy credits on Credits tab; wait a minute after payment |
| Payment succeeded but credits missing | Refresh the page; wait up to a minute; check Credits / payment history |
| Download missing on phone | Check Downloads / Files app; try **Copy Raw** and paste into Notes |
| Long wait after Generate | Long audio needs more time; keep the tab open |
| Wrong language / poor quality | Record closer to speakers; reduce noise; try Redo |
